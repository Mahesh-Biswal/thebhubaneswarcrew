The crew

One City.
Infinite Stories.

We are creators, comedians, poets and listeners who believe every voice deserves a stage — and Bhubaneswar deserves to be heard.

Why we exist

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To make Bhubaneswar a creative capital — a city where art, laughter and honest words are part of everyday life. A city where a student, a software engineer and a tea-stall owner can all share the same mic.

Our Mission

To discover and amplify local creators, build open stages for podcasts, comedy and poetry, and grow a community that shows up for each other — one episode, one open mic and one story at a time.

What we stand for

Our Values

Creativity

We chase fresh ideas and original voices over safe and familiar ones.

Community

Everyone has a seat at our table — listeners, first-timers and veterans alike.

Courage

Stepping on stage for the first time is brave. We honour and nurture that bravery.

Growth

We grow together — better episodes, sharper sets and stronger friendships.
