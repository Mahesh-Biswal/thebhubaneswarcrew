Our story

Built by Creators, For the City

The Bhubaneswar Crew began as a small circle of friends recording conversations in a cramped bedroom. Today it is a growing community of podcasters, comedians, poets and storytellers who believe every voice deserves a stage.

Our vision: to make Bhubaneswar a creative capital — a city where art, laughter and honest words are part of everyday life. Our mission: to discover local creators, amplify their stories, and build a stage that is open to everyone.

  • Weekly podcast episodes with local & visiting guests
  • Monthly open mics, comedy nights & poetry evenings
  • A home for creators to grow and collaborate
